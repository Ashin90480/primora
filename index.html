About the conclave

PRIMORA 26

PRIMORA is a techno-cultural event for first years organised by the IEEE Student Branch of John Cox Memorial CSI Institute of Technology. It brings together students, professionals and enthusiasts from the college to explore what's next in technology — and to unwind through music, performances and open floor sessions once the workshops wrap.

Expert workshops, expert talk sessions,live competitions, and a cultural stage that runs through both days.

The host branch

IEEE SB JCMCSIIT

Being one of the most active technical society student branches at John Cox Memorial CSI Institute of Technology, IEEE SB JCMCSIIT has played a vital role in developing research, robotics and technology culture on campus. The branch runs workshops, talks and competitions year-round, connecting student engineers with the wider IEEE network across Kerala — PRIMORA is its an techno-cultural Event for first years.
